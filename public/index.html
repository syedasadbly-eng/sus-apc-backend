Calibrated against 68 service days of real history (54,704 records), replayed through the same occupancy model this service runs. Sensor health, lone traveller, end of service and the dwell proxy are enabled, measuring 1.34 events per service day. R1 Dwell is a proxy: the VS125 reports counts, not identities, so it measures time held with nobody alighting, not how long any individual has been aboard. The sustain windows were widened to get there — 30 min for lone traveller, 60 min at a depot — from 23.71/day at the original 5 min. R9 Stationary stays off because speed is 0 in every record, which makes its movement test permanently true. End of service will raise nothing until GNSS push is enabled on the UR35s, as it requires a live fix and both buses report a fallback position. Occupancy is a modelled tally with a p90 error of 55 passengers, so treat these two rules as leads rather than observations.
